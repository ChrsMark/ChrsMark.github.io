About me

Christos Markou is a Principal Software Engineer and CNCF Ambassador with deep expertise in systems observability, distributed tracing, and cloud-native technologies.

With a background in Electrical and Computer Engineering from NTUA, he has built observability platforms, led open source initiatives, and spoken at global conferences.

Passionate about observability and the cloud-native ecosystem, he has contributed to widely used projects like Elastic Beats and OpenTelemetry Collector Contrib. Christos is an OpenTelemetry Collector Contrib Maintainer and actively works on Semantic Conventions stability in systems and Kubernetes areas.

Committed to open collaboration and community-driven evolution, he enjoys sharing insights through blog posts and speaking at conferences and events, engaging with users and the broader community.
